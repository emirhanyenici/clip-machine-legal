Privacy Policy

Clip Machine Uploader · Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who we are

Clip Machine Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool run by a single individual on their own computer. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What the app does

The app uploads short video clips that the owner has created and manually approved to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. It never posts without the owner's approval.

Data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and the permission to upload or publish videos (video.upload, video.publish).
  • YouTube / Instagram: only the permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel or account.

The app does not read your followers, messages, comments, contacts, location or any other personal data.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local configuration file. Upload results (video ID and link) are kept in local files. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the app.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Videos and their captions are sent only to the platform they are being uploaded to.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect the app at any time in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services), in your Google account permissions, or in Instagram's "Apps and websites". Disconnecting immediately invalidates the stored token; the local copy is deleted when the owner removes it from the configuration file.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
